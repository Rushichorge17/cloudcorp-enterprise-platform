
# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project Name

CloudCorp Enterprise Employee Management Platform

## Executive Summary

CloudCorp Technologies currently manages employee information using Microsoft Excel spreadsheets. As the company continues to grow, the existing process has become difficult to manage, less secure, and time-consuming.

The objective of this project is to design and implement a secure, scalable, cloud-based Employee Management Platform that centralizes employee information and automates routine HR activities. The new system should improve productivity, enhance security, reduce manual effort, and support future business growth.


## Company Background

CloudCorp Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a growing IT services company with approximately 200 employees. The company expects continuous growth over the next few years and requires an employee management solution that can scale with business requirements.

Currently, employee records are managed manually using Microsoft Excel spreadsheets. As the organization expands, this process becomes increasingly difficult to maintain.


## Current Business Problems

The organization currently faces the following challenges:

- Employee information is stored manually in Excel spreadsheets.
- Searching and updating records requires significant manual effort.
- Multiple users cannot efficiently collaborate on the same data.
- Data security and access control are limited.
- Reporting is time-consuming.
- The current process is difficult to scale as the company grows.
- Backup and recovery processes are not standardized.
- Human errors can result in incorrect or lost information.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Create a centralized employee management system.
- Automate manual HR processes to improve productivity.
- Improve the security of employee information.
- Implement role-based authentication and authorization.
- Simplify employee data management.
- Reduce human errors caused by manual processes.
- Build a scalable platform that supports future business growth.
- Improve system availability and reliability.
- Enable employees and HR staff to securely access the system from anywhere.
- Build a cloud-native solution that is cost-effective and easy to maintain.
